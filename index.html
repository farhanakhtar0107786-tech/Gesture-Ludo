✋ Gesture controls
  1. Show 1–5 fingers to choose dice 1–5.
  2. Show a closed fist to choose 6.
  3. Keep the gesture steady for about 1.1 seconds.
  4. Remove your hand from the camera.
  5. Show 1–4 fingers to select a numbered piece.
  6. The piece moves automatically if the move is valid.

Use one hand, face your palm toward the camera and keep fingers separated. For 1–4, keep the thumb folded.
